# Race Items

A community race option (`Race.race_items`) that gives a runner a Mario Kart style item for each boss kill. The item hurts other runners, and the rank decides which items a runner can draw: mild or defensive ones for the leader, punitive ones for the runners at the back.

This document is the operational reference for the server side (rules, lifecycle, inventory reconciliation and persistence) and the mod side (flags, load gating, effects, overlay). The wire messages are in `PROTOCOL.md`; how the mod reports use flags and its inventory is in `EVENT_FLAG_TRACKING.md`.

---

## Rules

- On for community races only: never in Daily Seeds, speedrun runs (Set Seed, Random Seed), solo training sessions or event races (the admin attach of a race with items to an event slot is a 422).
- On the race page, the Race Rules card states the option in one line, with the item icons under it (`RaceItemsLegend.svelte`): pointing at, focusing or tapping an icon describes the item and the ranks that draw it on a line below. The list is `RACE_ITEMS` in `web/src/lib/raceItems.ts`, static and hand-kept: it shows the latest catalog whatever the race's seed carries, so update it with the generator's catalog (names, durations), `ITEM_RULES` (effects) and `DEFAULT_RACE_ITEMS_TIER_WEIGHTS` (tiers).
- On `/race/new`, the option is the "Elden Drift" tile of the Format field (the community's name for an items race; Classic, Elden Drift, Speedrun: one format per race, so items never meet a speedrun race; no Format field on a Set Seed race), carrying a gold "New" tag over its top right corner, as the "Recommended" one of the game mode tabs. Selected, the panel under the tiles pitches the format, shows the item icons (`RaceItemsLegend.svelte`, as on the race page) and says such races count on no board. The game mode tabs then count the seeds an items race draws (`race_items_available` of `GET /api/pools`): a pool without one is disabled, a selected one reads "No seed with race items here yet" and Create Race is off, and with none anywhere the form says "No seed with race items in any game mode yet." The heading reads "Create Elden Drift Race". Picking the format sets the late-join window to 5 minutes, enough to join a race seen starting (30 again on another format); a window the organizer typed is left alone, the default never goes past the auto-end duration, and the format's panel says "Late joiners get 5 minutes by default (see the advanced options)." The name is the format's on this form only: the rules, the race page and the game keep "race items".
- A seed supports items when `graph_json["plugins"]["race_items"]["enabled"]` is true and `graph_json["race_items"]` (the catalog, `{goods, items}` since graph.json 4.12) holds at least one valid item; the pool scan stores the answer in `Seed.race_items`. A seed generated before 4.12 (a flat `{item_key: entry}` catalog, one goods per item) supports none: the migration `24e50b1ae2f9` cleared `Seed.race_items` on those already scanned, so items races no longer draw them. An items race only draws seeds that support them.
- Switching the option on in SETUP over a seed without the plugin redraws the seed (`apply_seed_reroll` with `count_as_reroll=False`: the option did it, not a player, so it is no reroll for the boards); with no seed to draw it is a 400 and the option stays off.
- Any change of the option (on or off, with or without a redraw) closes every connected mod of the race with code 1000, as a race reset does: the catalog and the protocol gate below only apply at auth, so each mod reconnects and authenticates again, gets the catalog that matches, and an older mod meets the gate. Spectators stay connected.
- A runner holds at most one item. A boss kill made while holding one grants nothing, then or later.
- Only playing runners are ever targets; finished, abandoned and disqualified runners are skipped.
- An items race needs a mod speaking protocol 1.18 or later (`RACE_ITEMS_MIN_PROTOCOL`); older mods are refused at auth.
- Items races stay out of the speedrun boards, the community stats and the generator calibration, since items distort clear times and deaths.
- Items and deathless can be combined; nothing validates the choice.

## Item order

Draws and targets rank the playing runners in item order (`item_standings` in `server/speedfog_racing/websocket/race/items.py`, `item_order` in `services/race_items.py`). It starts from the leaderboard order (`sort_leaderboard`: deepest layer, then layer entry IGT), then each runner moves up past every runner directly above it that it leads by `race_items_igt_lead_s` (60 s) or more in IGT at the same point, and stops at the first it does not. The lead is the difference of their `gap_ms` (`leaderboard_gaps` in `websocket/race/manager.py`, the gaps the leaderboard shows: IGT behind the leaderboard leader on the same layer, LiveSplit style). A runner without a gap neither moves nor is passed.

Why: the mod freezes IGT during loading fades, so time lost outside IGT (slow loads, a crash and relaunch) leaves a runner on a shallower layer while it may be ahead in IGT, which ranks the race. A study of 501 finished simultaneous races (2026-11-14, finishers only, the time each lost outside IGT spread over its loads, sampled every minute) found the two orders differ at 29% of the instants, mostly by small leads (25 s at the median), with 1.6% of runner pairs inverted by 60 s or more and 17% of races holding an inversion of 2 min or more at some point. Where they disagree, the IGT order matched the final ranking 64% of the time, and 86% for leads of 60 s or more. The threshold keeps the order the runners see on the board unless the IGT lead is a real one, so a Red Shell rarely hits someone other than the runner listed just above.

## Tiers

Among the playing runners, in item order (index `i` of `n`):

| Tier   | Runners                                |
| ------ | -------------------------------------- |
| front  | `n <= 2`: index 0; otherwise `3*i < n` |
| middle | `3*i < 2*n` (only when `n > 2`)        |
| back   | the rest (with `n <= 2`: index 1)      |

## Items

Catalog keys come from the seed (`graph.json` `race_items`, read by `extract_race_item_catalog` in `server/speedfog_racing/services/race_items.py`): `goods` by goods key (`goods_id`, `use_flag`, `remove_flag`, `icon`, `icon_id`) and `items` by item key (`name`, `icon`, `icon_id`, `goods`, `effect_speffect`, `duration_ms`). Every usable item is held as the one Race Item goods (`race_item`, use flag `1050290101`); the Shield, never used, is held as its own goods (`shield`, no use flag). An item on a goods the catalog lacks is dropped. The rules (target, effect, banner wording) live in `ITEM_RULES` in the same module. Positions (just ahead, just behind, best-ranked) are in item order.

| Item                            | Tiers (default) | Target                         | Effect                                                                                   |
| ------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

The Talisman Thief, the Junk Weapon and the Hunter need data blocks of graph.json 4.14 beside `goods` and `items`: `race_items.talismans` (`{id, name, flag}`), `race_items.junk_weapons` (`{id, name}`) and `race_items.hunter` (`{chr_id, think_param, npc_params}`, `npc_params[t - 1]` being the NpcParam row of tier `t`). `extract_race_item_catalog` leaves an item out of `items` when its block is missing, empty or malformed (a debug log line: every catalog parse of an older seed says it), so a seed generated before 4.14 never draws it. The talisman flags come from the seed: nobody recomputes them.

**Deploy.** The Hunter's NpcParam and NpcThinkParam rows are written into the seed's regulation by the generator's FogModWrapper, while its graph.json block comes from the generator's Python side: re-run `tools/bootstrap.py` on the generation host (it republishes FogModWrapper) before regenerating the pools, then check one regenerated seed offline with speedfog's `tools/game_inspect`: `dump-param <regulation.bin> NpcParam --row 1450850` (also `--row 1450883`) and `dump-param <regulation.bin> NpcThinkParam --row 1450850` must each print the row, not `Row ... not found`. A seed built by a stale FogModWrapper lacks them: the mod then misses every Hunter there (`Hunter not requested: row missing from the regulation`, see Hunter) and the sender reads `The Hunter lost the trail`.

The Star's effect is a SpEffect row, and so is the Lightning's slow walk (the mod adds its shrink); Vanish, Unseen Foes, Slow-mo, Giant, the Camera, the Talisman Thief, the Junk Weapon and the Hunter have none (`effect_speffect` null): the mod applies them itself (see Effects), from the `item_effect` kinds listed in `docs/PROTOCOL.md`. The Camera's variant (`first_person`, `inverted` or `wide`, uniform) is drawn by the server at each use, so the target's banner, the target's reminder and the sender's result name the same one.
The draw is weighted inside the tier's pool: `race_items_tier_weights` maps each tier to its item weights (base key to weight), so an item can sit in several tiers; the pool is the tier's items with a weight above 0 that the seed's catalog has. The Ink is left out of the pool when no other playing runner can still meet a zone with unused exits, the only zones where the mod runs one (`ink_can_land`: the runner's `current_zone` or a node reachable from it along the graph's `edges` has unused exits, any count but 0, as the mod reads it; a count unknown, on a seed that predates graph.json 4.13, or a runner not placed yet counts as one), checked only when the drawn tier holds the Ink, logged `race item draw: ink left out` with the tier: the draw goes on among the tier's other items. When the pool is empty nothing is granted (a `race item draw: nothing to draw` warning, no `item_grant_count` bump, no event). A draw of `rune_pouch` becomes the variant matching progress: `current_layer / total_layers` below 1/3 gives `rune_pouch_1`, below 2/3 `rune_pouch_2`, else `rune_pouch_3`. A drawn key absent from the seed's catalog grants nothing (logged).

**Reported items.**

The Talisman Thief and the Hunter play out in the target's game, so the server learns the outcome from the target's mod (`item_report`), the round trip below. At the use, the target (Star and Shield blocks and offline targets as for any item) gets an `item_effect` carrying a `report_id` and the sender's result reads `Hit {name}`; the server registers a `PendingReport` (kind `steal` or `hunter`, sender, target, item, time) in `RaceRoom.pending_reports`, in memory under `items_lock`, with ids from `RaceRoom.next_report_id` (one counter for the whole process, started from the clock in milliseconds, so neither a room made again for its race nor a restarted server issues an id twice).

- **Talisman Thief.** The effect (`steal_talisman`) has no banner and carries the seed's whole talisman table (`{id, flag}`) and a `roll` (`rng.getrandbits(32)`) the victim's mod uses to pick among the talismans it wears. The victim's mod removes the talisman and answers `item_report {report_id, landed, talisman_id}`. Landed with a `talisman_id` of the table: the thief gets a `talisman` effect with the banner `Talisman Thief` / `Stole {talisman}` / `from {victim}` and the victim an `item_notice` `Talisman Thief` / `{talisman} stolen` / `by {thief}`. Anything else (nothing worn, a talisman outside the table) counts as not landed: the thief gets an `item_notice` `Talisman Thief` / `Nothing to steal` / `{victim} wears no talisman`. A thief whose mod is gone at the report loses the talisman (logged warning; the event still reads `stolen`).
- **Junk Weapon.** The weapon is drawn uniformly from `junk_weapons` at each use and sent as `junk_weapon {weapon_id}` with the banner `{weapon} forced into your right hand` / `from {sender}`; the result detail is the weapon name. No report: the banner and the sender's result go out whatever the target's mod manages (an unresolved `ChangeEquipment` or a full inventory is only logged in its mod log, see Equipment).
- **Hunter.** The effect (`hunter`) carries `chr_id`, `think_id` (the seed's `hunter`), `duration_ms` (15 s, from the catalog entry; an entry without it sends no effect, logged, as the memory kinds) and `npc_param_id`, the row of the tier of the leader's node (`nodes[current_zone].tier`, clamped to `1..len(npc_params)`; a leader with no current zone or a node without a tier gets tier 1, logged). Banner `Margit hunts you for {s}s` / `from {sender}`, reminder `Hunted` (the mod shows both once he is found); the result detail is `{s}s`. The mod reports whether he spawned: not landed sends the sender an `item_notice` `Hunter` / `The Hunter lost the trail` / `{leader}`, landed sends nothing. His return after a quit-out (see Effects) sends no second report.

`resolve_item_report` answers a report only from its effect's target, once (the entry is spent by the first answer, whatever it says) and within 300 s of the effect: an unknown or foreign id, a second report or a late one changes nothing. Entries older than 300 s are dropped when a new one is registered. Each answered report logs a `report` event (`item` the item key, actor the sender, `igt_ms` the target's IGT at the report, `targets` `[{"participant_id": target, "outcome": "stolen" | "nothing" | "spawned" | "missed", "talisman_id"?}]`) and an INFO line `Race item report answered` (race, report id, kind, outcome and the talisman id reported).

### Damage curve

For banana and red shell, with `h` the target's current HP as a percentage of max HP and `B` the base damage: `h >= floor + B` deals `B`; `tail + floor <= h < floor + B` deals `h - floor` (lands at the floor); below that it deals `tail`; at least 1 HP always remains. `floor` is `race_items_damage_floor_pct` (20) and `tail` is `race_items_damage_tail_pct` (10); the bases are `race_items_red_shell_pct` (40) and `race_items_banana_pct` (15). The mod computes the damage when it applies the effect, since only it knows the current HP; the parameters travel with the effect.

## Lifecycle

1. **Boss kill.** The boss kill hook (`BaseModHandler._on_boss_killed`) fires once per new kill, after the commit and the broadcast, outside any DB session. It never fires for the final boss, a replay, a re-report, a kill at IGT 0 or less, or a kill whose node has no visit.
2. **Draw.** `grant_item` (`server/speedfog_racing/websocket/race/items.py`) opens its own session and loads the field. It grants nothing when items are off, the runner is not playing, or the runner holds an item. Otherwise it picks the tier from the runner's rank in item order, draws the key, bumps `Participant.item_grant_count`, stores the held item as unconfirmed with the kill's IGT, logs a `grant` event and commits.
3. **Grant.** The server sends `item_grant {grant_id, item, banner}` to the runner's own mod. The grant id counts up per participant and never repeats.
4. **Confirm.** The mod writes the item's icon into its goods' row, spawns the goods (the Race Item, or the Shield's own goods) and answers `item_received {grant_id}`; the server sets `confirmed` when the id matches the held grant, and ignores anything else.
5. **Reconnect.** `auth_ok` carries `held_item`, the item held (confirmed or not), so a restarted mod restores the Race Item's icon and the held row. An unconfirmed grant is re-sent right after `auth_ok` at every (re)auth, and after every inventory report until it is confirmed, the report that confirms it included (see below). The mod ignores a grant id it already has.
6. **Use.** Using the Race Item makes the plugin's EMEVD set its use flag, the same for every item, which the mod reports at once through `event_flag`. The handler matches use flags (the `use_flag` of each catalog goods, `use_flag_map`: use flag to goods key, cached per graph) after the usual gates (race running, no countdown, runner playing, initialized history, plausible IGT) and before the `event_map` lookup, and never adds a `zone_history` entry. After the session, the `_on_item_used` hook resolves the use; it does nothing when the race has items off, and outside races (training) it is a no-op. The flag is acked once the hook has resolved the use, so a use whose resolution never committed (a server restart, or a resolution that failed: logged `Race item use failed`, the flag left unacked) is replayed by the mod at its next reconnection. The item is still held then, unless an inventory report cleared it as `lost` meanwhile (the game consumed the goods at the use).
7. **Resolve.** `resolve_item_use` (same module) acts only when the runner holds an item of the used goods, whatever item it is: the held item says what the use does. Otherwise it logs the use and ignores it, and a goods without a use flag (the Shield) is never used. That covers a stray copy, and the replay of a resolved use whose ACK was lost, since the resolution cleared the held item. A use older in IGT than the held item's grant (`held_item.igt_ms`, the kill's IGT) is ignored too: made before the kill that granted the item, it can only be the replay of an earlier use, so a replay never spends an item granted since (any item: they share the goods). It clears the held item and resolves the targets among the playing runners in item order. For each target:
   - a target inside its Star window (below) is blocked first: it gets `item_notice` with a banner, nothing is spent and a Shield it holds stays;
   - a held Shield blocks the item: the Shield is spent (even when its holder is offline) and its holder gets `item_remove` for the `shield` goods with a banner;
   - otherwise a target whose mod is connected gets `item_effect` (effect, banner, reminder);
   - otherwise the target is offline and the effect is dropped, a miss for the sender.

   A target counts as hit when its mod is connected at resolution, before the send: if that send then fails (the socket died meanwhile), the effect is lost but the sender's result still reads `Hit {name}` and the log says `hit`.

   The Blue Shell's target is the leader at the time of use, the sender included: drawn at the back and used once leading, it hits its own sender (as in Mario Kart), who then gets both the `item_effect` (banner `from yourself`) and a result reading `Hit yourself`.

   A self item (Mushroom, Rune Pouch, Star) has no target: its effect row (`effect_speffect`: the Mushroom's stamina, the Rune Pouch's runes, the Star's invincibility) goes to the sender's own mod as an `item_effect` without banner or reminder, which its result carries. An item with no valid target is still consumed. One `use` event is logged with every target's outcome (`hit`, `blocked` or `offline`; none for a self item).

   The Star is a self item that also opens a window: using it sets `star_until[sender] = now + duration_ms / 1000` (`now` is `time.monotonic()`; `RaceRoom.star_until`, in memory only, used under `items_lock`), and a target whose deadline is still ahead of `now` is blocked, connected or not. The window covers only the Star's user and ends with the effect. The block is logged `blocked` like a Shield's. The window runs on wall time while the mod times the invincibility on its play clock, which a load pauses: a fog gate taken during the Star ends the window that much before the invincibility.

8. **Result.** The sender gets `item_result` (banner, plus the reminder for a self item). The handler sends the removals (a Shield spent), the notices (a Star holder's block), the effects (a self item's to its sender), then the result.
9. **Report.** A Talisman Thief or Hunter use is not final until its target's `item_report` (see Reported items); the handler (`_handle_item_report`) runs it under `items_lock` and sends the notices, then the effects.

Grants, confirmations and uses run under `RaceRoom.items_lock`, so concurrent connections cannot grant, confirm or spend over each other: two items aimed at one Shield holder spend the Shield once. A grant after a kill and a use also send their messages under the lock, so a later item message to a runner cannot overtake them.

### Inventory reconciliation

The mod sends `item_inventory {goods}` (the race goods present in the game, by goods key) once per game load, after that load's first flag poll and before any pending spawn, and once per reconnect. The server treats it as the truth about the goods, under the same lock; the held item shows as its goods, and keys absent from the catalog's goods are ignored:

| Held item   | Report                | Outcome                                                                                 |
| ----------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| unconfirmed | its goods present     | confirmed, and its `item_grant` re-sent once (see below)                                |
| confirmed   | its goods absent      | cleared and logged `lost`, so the runner can draw again                                 |
| unconfirmed | its goods absent      | kept, its goods may not be spawned yet: its `item_grant` is re-sent                     |
| any or none | another goods present | `item_remove {goods}` without banner sent, logged `take_back` (the goods key as `item`) |

Since every usable item shares the Race Item, a Race Item reported while the server holds an unconfirmed usable item confirms it, though the copy may have been granted as another item (a `give` over a held item, a stale copy). The grant re-sent with the confirmation tells the mod which item the copy now holds: the mod skips the spawn (the goods is there), writes the item's icon, shows its row and answers `item_received`, a no-op by then.

### Result and block banners

The banner title is the catalog `name`. The sender's result line:

| Outcome              | Line                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| hits                 | `Hit {name}`, or `Hit {n} runners`; with Shield or Star blocks too, `, {k} blocked` is appended |
| Blocks only          | `Blocked by {name}'s shield`, `Blocked by {name}'s Star`, or `Blocked by {k} runners`           |
| no target            | `Missed: nobody ahead` (Red Shell), `Missed: nobody behind` (Banana), `Missed: no other runner` |
| targets, all offline | `Missed`                                                                                        |
| self item            | `Infinite stamina for {s} s` (Mushroom), `Invincible for {s} s` (Star), `Runes added` (Pouch)   |

The result detail is `{pct}% HP` for a damage item, `{s} s` for a timed one, else empty; it stays empty when nothing landed (every target blocked, missed or offline), so it never reads as dealt. A target's banner reads `from {sender}` under the item's target line. A Shield holder whose Shield blocked sees title `Shield`, line `Blocked {item name}`, detail `from {sender}`; a Star holder's Star block sends an `item_notice` with title `Star` and the same line and detail. The Camera's detail reads `{reminder}, {s} s`.

The new reported items add: Talisman Thief, to the thief `Stole {talisman}` / `from {victim}`, to the victim `{talisman} stolen` / `by {thief}` (an `item_notice`), to the thief again `Nothing to steal` / `{victim} wears no talisman`; Junk Weapon `{weapon} forced into your right hand`, result detail the weapon name; Hunter `Margit hunts you for {s}s` / `Hunted`, to its sender `The Hunter lost the trail` / `{leader}`. Their grant lines are `Steals a talisman from the runner ahead`, `Forces a junk weapon on the runner ahead` and `Sends Margit after the leader for {s}s`.

Target lines and reminders of the memory items, and of the Lightning (`Slowed and shrunk for {s} s` / `Slowed and shrunk`): Vanish `You are invisible for {s} s` / `Invisible`; Unseen Foes `Enemies unseen for {s} s` / `Unseen foes`; Slow-mo `Slowed down for {s} s` / `Slow-mo` (the speed is a setting); Giant `Giant for {s} s` / `Giant`; Camera per variant `First-person camera for {s} s` / `First person`, `Upside-down camera for {s} s` / `Upside down`, `Wide camera for {s} s` / `Wide camera`; Star reminder `Invincible`.

## Persistence

- `Participant.held_item`: `{"grant_id": int, "item": str, "confirmed": bool, "igt_ms": int}` or null, `igt_ms` being the IGT of the grant (the boss kill; the runner's IGT for a `give` of the bot tool), read as 0 when absent (rows written before it). The column is JSON: reassign a new dict, never mutate in place.
- `Participant.item_grant_count`: grants handed out so far; a reset keeps it, so ids never repeat.
- Pending reports (`RaceRoom.pending_reports`) live in memory for 5 minutes: a server restart forgets them, and a report that arrives after is ignored (a stolen talisman is then lost to the thief, but the victim's slot is already empty). Their ids come from one counter for the whole process, started from the clock in milliseconds: a room dropped and made again, or a restarted server, never issues an id again, so a report a mod still queues from before matches no new effect.
- `race_item_events`: the item log (`race_id`, `participant_id`, `kind`, `item`, `igt_ms`, `targets`, `created_at`); `kind` is `grant`, `use`, `report`, `lost` or `take_back`, and `item` is the item key (the goods key for a `take_back`). The actor (`participant_id`) of a `report` is the item's sender, and its `igt_ms` the target's IGT at the report (the target and its outcome are in `targets`). Kept for audits and later web use.

## Server settings

`race_items_tier_weights`, `race_items_banana_pct`, `race_items_red_shell_pct`, `race_items_damage_floor_pct`, `race_items_damage_tail_pct`, `race_items_hammer_load_pct`, `race_items_slow_mo_speed_pct` (50, clamped to 10..100), `race_items_lightning_scale_pct` (50, the Lightning's size) and `race_items_giant_scale_pct` (150), both clamped to 25..300, and `race_items_igt_lead_s` (the item order's lead, see Item order) in `config.py`, overridable from `.env`. They travel with the effects, so tuning needs neither a mod release nor new seeds. `RACE_ITEMS_TIER_WEIGHTS` (JSON) is laid over the default table: a partial table only overrides the tiers it names (a tier named replaces that tier's weights whole, so an override written before an item was added never draws it in the tiers it names), the others keep their default. A tier other than `front`, `middle` and `back`, or a negative weight, stops the server from starting with a settings error; an item key no rule knows (`ITEM_RULES`) is never drawn and logged as a warning at startup. The percentages are clamped to 0..100 when they are sent (the mod reads each as a byte; the Hammer's to 0..99, since 100 could round past the max), and the banners quote the clamped value.

## Exclusions

Items races are kept out of the speedrun boards (an unranked reason in `race_unranked_code` and `counts_on_boards_clause`), the community stats and traits (`counts_for_community_stats`) and the generator calibration queries of `tools/extract_zone_times.py`. Race rewards and highlights still apply.

## Mod

The mod acts only while the race has `race_items` on (`RaceInfo.race_items`), the seed carries a `race_item_catalog` and the run is live (race running, runner not finished or abandoned).

### Flags

Each catalog goods has a goods id and event flags in category 1050290 (one page resolve for all of them): the Race Item a use flag (`1050290101`) and a removal flag (`1050290201`), the Shield only a removal flag (`1050290200`).

- **Use flag.** The Race Item's, whatever item it holds. Read on the same scans as the boss flags (10 Hz poll, loading exit, reconnect rescan), each scan. A set use flag is cleared in game at once and sent through `event_flag` with its `message_id`, ACK and replay, like a boss flag. It never joins `triggered_flags` (it fires again on the next use) and is no LiveSplit capture. The game consumed the goods: the mod forgets the item held as it, and its held row goes (a Shield, held as its own goods, stays).
- **Remove flag.** Only ever raised by the mod (`item_remove {goods}`, never on a wrong save); the game's own event clears it and the mod never does. The item held as that goods, and its row, go with it; an item held as the other goods stays. A removal raised during a load can fail silently: the inventory report at the exit of that same load, seconds later, still lists the goods and gets it taken back again (see Inventory reconciliation).

### Load gating

Grants and effects wait until the player is loaded on the race save (no wrong save, no outdated seed pack), then go on the first loaded frame, after its flag scan (use flags sent): each grant's icon write and spawn (answered with `item_received`), then the effects. Once per game load (loading exit) and once per reconnect frame, while connected, the held item's icon write and the `item_inventory` report come first, in this order: flag scan, icon, report, grants, effects applied again (a loading exit only, see Effects), new effects; a loading exit while disconnected writes no icon and sends no report, the reconnect frame does both. A spawn that fails is retried at the next load or reconnect, and so is one the shell never answered (its batch lost to a frame that panicked; warning `[ITEMS] Grant spawns never answered`). A grant still waiting (for its load, or for that retry) is dropped when the server no longer holds it: an `item_remove` drops the queued grants of its goods, a new grant drops every older one still queued (the server only grants to a runner holding nothing), and an `auth_ok` drops those of any item but the `held_item` it names (an `item_remove` sent while the mod was offline is lost), so a goods spent or taken back before it spawned does not appear after (`[ITEMS] Queued grants dropped`). The time of an effect (its `duration_ms`), its banner (none for the runner's own self item, whose `item_result` carries it) and its reminder start when it is applied.

Timers run on the play clock (`core/play_clock.rs`): wall time while the player's position is readable (the signal the loading exit keys on) on the race save, so loading screens, the main menu and a wrong save stop it (a malus would otherwise run out there at no in-game cost; the wrong save is detected after the clock's update, so it stops a frame late at worst). It times the effects, the reminder rows and the ink; banners keep wall time. Once the run is over (race no longer running, runner finished or out), the grants, failed spawns and effects still waiting are dropped, not kept for a later load, and the timed effects are forgotten (the game runs out the ones it holds, under their reminder rows, none comes back at a load); a new start (a race reset run again) drops them too, with the reminder rows and the ink of the voided run, and so does an authentication as another participant. The memory effects go the same way, their reminder rows with them (the Lightning's row is its slow walk's, a timed one, so it stays although the shrink goes), and the next loaded frame writes the game's defaults back once (see Effects). A new start also forgets the uses of the voided run still unacked: replayed into the new run, one could pass for a use of an item granted since. So does the Hunter: one requested and not missed yet, hunting, or still watched after his last miss is ended at once (`EndHunter`, wherever the player is), his `Hunted` row with him, and one not requested yet is forgotten, with no report.

### Effects

- `speffect`: the SpEffect is applied to the player. `duration_ms` is set for a timed effect (Mushroom, Star, Blue Shell, Flask Stopper) and absent for an instant one (Rune Pouch, Toxic Blossom).
- `equip_load` (the Hammer): the mod first sets the SpEffect row's max equip load rate so the player's current load sits at `load_pct`% of the max (97 by default, a fat roll), then applies it (`duration_ms` carries its time). Applied again at a loading exit, it comes back as its SpEffect alone: the rate written at its application stays in its row. The current load is the sum of the weights of the equipped weapons, armor and talismans (`ChrAsm.equipment_param_ids` at PlayerGameData + 0x398, read from EquipParamWeapon, EquipParamProtector and EquipParamAccessory through libeldenring's in-memory params, a weapon's row dropping its upgrade level); arrows and bolts are left out, their param weight is not a load weight (10 for plain Arrows); the max is PlayerGameData + 0x98C, the equipment screen's value. The rate is capped at 1, so a runner already that heavy is left alone, and a runner with nothing equipped (load 0) cannot be made heavier: both still see the banner and reminder. A running Hammer points at the same row, so the max read while it runs already includes the rate written last: when the max equals the last base times the last rate, the mod computes from that base (`core::equip_load::base_max`), so a second Hammer follows the current equipment instead of writing 1 and cancelling the first. An unreadable load, or params not indexed yet (`prewarm_params`), keeps the row's rate (the seed's 0.35, or the last one written). The log line `[ITEMS] Equip load rate set` gives `current` and `max`, to compare with the equipment screen.
- `damage`: computed when applied, with `h = 100 * hp / max_hp`: `h >= floor + base` deals `base`% of max HP; `h >= floor + tail` deals down to exactly `floor`%; otherwise `tail`%; at least 1 HP always remains. Max is the effective max HP (halved under a Blue Shell).
- `ink`: overlay only, see below. It runs once the runner's zone has `unused_exits` above 0 (the zone where it hit included), and waits until then; in a boss arena with unused exits whose boss lives, it hides the exits at once and its time runs from the kill (see Overlay). At the hit, the newest zone decides: the one a pending reveal brings (its `zone_update` is in, the fade still covers it), else the current one. An ink applied on a loading exit frame always waits: the destination's `zone_update` has not come yet (the fog flags deferred to that exit go out on that same frame) and its reveal waits for the fade, so the next reveal decides.
- `lightning` (`speffect_id`, `scale_pct`, `duration_ms`): the Lightning. Its SpEffect, a forced slow walk, is applied as a timed `speffect` (applied again with its time left after a load), and the player is scaled to `scale_pct` as by `scale`, both for `duration_ms`. A scale running (the Giant) is replaced, its row with it; a Giant that comes later replaces the shrink, while the `Slowed and shrunk` row stays with the slow walk until its end.
- `vanish`, `unseen_foes`, `slow_mo` (`speed_pct`), `scale` (`scale_pct`: the Giant) and `camera` (`variant`): memory effects, with no SpEffect row. Each carries `duration_ms`, which the mod requires (it drops the whole message without it): the server sends none for an item whose catalog entry lacks it, logs a warning (`effect_payload`), and the use reads as missed (a target not blocked is logged `offline`). The mod keeps each on the play clock (`core/memory_effects.rs`, one entry per kind: a second hit of a kind, a Giant on a Giant (or on the Lightning's shrink) or a Camera on a Camera, keeps the later end and shows the new value; its reminder row replaces the previous one's, the machine keeping the last reminder text per kind, so a Camera then another variant shows the later one's row alone. The Lightning's shrink keeps no reminder text: its row is its slow walk's, which a Giant leaves in place, see `lightning` above) and derives from the live ones the state the game should hold (`MemoryState`): the player hidden (a Vanish, or a first-person Camera), the foes hidden (Unseen Foes), the animation speed (`speed_pct` / 100), the scale (`scale_pct` / 100) and the camera variant. Two effects on one field hold it until the last one ends: a Vanish ending under a first-person Camera keeps the player hidden until the Camera ends. On a loaded frame of the race save, the machine hands the state to the shell (`Effect::ApplyMemoryState`: every field written, an unset one as the game's default) when it changes, at each loading exit while one is live, and every 250 ms of wall time while one is live, so a field the game reset or a write that failed comes back; the write walks the characters around (`walk_foes`) when the foes are hidden or shown, at a loading exit while they are hidden, and every 1 s while they are hidden, for the characters loaded since. Once none is live (ended, or dropped with the run), the defaults are written once: the player and the foes shown, speed and scale 1, the vanilla camera, the foes walked back if they were hidden. Each change is logged (`[ITEMS] Memory state changed`, with the state and `walk_foes`); the shell then writes the fields (see Memory writes).
- `steal_talisman` (the Talisman Thief, no banner): the mod reads the player's four talisman slots and takes, among the talismans worn that the effect's table (`talismans`, the seed's) knows, in slot order, the one at `roll % count` (`core::talisman_thief::pick_talisman`: an empty slot, read -1, and a talisman outside the table are skipped). It raises that talisman's flag, whose event in the seed removes it, which empties its slot (the mod writes no equipment), then answers `item_report` landed with its `talisman_id`; nothing worn the table knows, or slots unreadable, answers not landed with a null `talisman_id`. The shell hands the outcome to `RaceMachine::talisman_steal_result`, which returns the report. A flag write that fails takes nothing (see Equipment).
- `talisman` (the thief's gift, after the victim's report): the mod gives the talisman `talisman_id` (`race_items::spawn_talisman`, see Equipment); its banner shows when applied.
- `junk_weapon`: the mod gives the weapon `weapon_id` and equips it in the right weapon slot in hand (`core::item_inventory::junk_slot`: ChrAsm slot `1 + 2 * right_weapon_slot`, a value above 2 read as the first slot). The copy equipped is the one the give added (`new_copy`: the entry of that weapon whose gaitem handle was not in the inventory before the give, the last acquired, highest `sort_id`, when several, logged), never the runner's own copy of that weapon, which may carry an Ash of War or sit in another slot. The weapon is matched at any upgrade level (`same_weapon`: the same id once its last two digits, the level, are dropped, so the same affinity): the Item Randomizer's auto-upgrade, on in SpeedFog seeds by default, raises a weapon given to the runner's best level as the game adds it, so the copy rarely has the +0 id sent. A right hand that already holds that weapon (`junk_is_free`: the same weapon and affinity, any upgrade level) is left alone. The equip goes through the game's own equip function (`eldenring/equip_change.rs`, see Equipment): a game version where the mod does not find it gives nothing, and neither does a version outside 1.17.x: the function is the same on 1.16.2, but the PlayerGameData offsets it is called with are 1.17.x's. No report: the banner shows whatever the shell manages, and a failure (the function unresolved, a full inventory) is only logged.
- `hunter` (`chr_id`, `npc_param_id`, `think_id`, `duration_ms`, `report_id`): Margit, one at a time (`core/hunter.rs`, ticked by the machine right after the memory effects, on loaded frames of the race save; the effect is applied as any other, then waits there). He is requested (`RequestHunter`) once the player has been loaded 2.5 s of wall time since the last loading exit (no loading exit seen since the mod started counts as settled), spawned 3 m behind the player, 0.5 m up. The mod then looks for him (`FindHunter`: a live character of his NpcParam row) every 250 ms for 3 s. Not found by then, or the spawn refused (`RaceMachine::hunter_request_result`), that request missed: he is ended (`EndHunter`: a spawn still pending cleared, every live character of his row brought to 0 HP, so a late arrival does not stay) and requested again 1.5 s later, up to 3 requests; the third miss is reported not landed (`item_report`, the server then tells the sender), and he is still looked for every second for 10 s after it: one that arrives then is ended at once (`EndHunter`), with no banner, no row and no other report. A new Hunter during those 10 s is sent out as usual. Found (`RaceMachine::hunter_seen`), he is reported landed, and his banner and reminder (`Hunted`), held until then, show on the next frame. He hunts for `duration_ms` of play time (a load does not count), looked for every second: a look that no longer finds him (a load removed him, or the runner killed him) ends the hunt there, with nothing to end, and his `Hunted` row goes at once; otherwise he is ended once his time is up, his row with him. A quit-out is the exception, so that reloading the save cannot shake him off (`Hunter::quit_out`, told at the loading exit by the machine's quit-out latch, the one that arms the quit-out penalty: an IGT regression of less than 60 s around an unload, see `docs/EVENT_FLAG_TRACKING.md`): the hunt and its row go on, and he is brought back once the player has settled, through the same requests (up to 3, 1.5 s apart, each looked for 3 s). Found, he hunts until the same end on the play clock (the quit-out's unload is not played time, and its IGT penalty does not touch that clock), with no new banner and no new report. Missed for good, or his time up on the way back, the hunt ends there with its row, with no report, and a request already made gets the 10 s watch for a late arrival. A quit-out while he is still awaited makes the interrupted request again, not counted. A death or a fog gate still ends the hunt, on his way back too: any other loading exit during his return ends it there (`Hunter::load`), quietly. A trip through another save between the quit-out and the race save's reload (a wrong save) is no quit-out to the latch, so it ends the hunt as well. A second Hunter while one is awaited, hunting or being brought back is reported not landed when applied, with no banner nor reminder. The machine's own work for him is a few comparisons per frame: the walks run only when it asks for a look, at most every 250 ms while a spawn is awaited and every second while he hunts or is watched after his last miss.
- `item_notice` (no effect): its banner shows at once, gold like a Shield's block banner (the Star holder's block); nothing is applied and nothing waits for a load.
- an unknown kind, or a `camera` variant the mod does not know, is ignored with a warning (`[ITEMS] Effect of an unknown kind or variant, dropped`), its banner and reminder with it.

**Across loads.** The game drops the SpEffects the mod applies at a load. The mod times each timed one it applied on the play clock (`core/timed_effects.rs`, one end per SpEffect id: a second hit keeps the later end) and, at each loading exit (a fog gate, a death, a quit-out reloaded), applies again every one with time left, with that time left (`[ITEMS] Effect applied again`, with the id and `left_ms`), before the effects that waited for that load: a hit received during a load applies the running copy again, then the new one, whose full time wins. This happens behind the same gates as the rest (run live, race save, current seed pack), connected or not: the effects are local. A loading exit on a wrong save applies nothing again; the race save's next loading exit does, with the time left it had there (the play clock stops on a wrong save). A reconnect frame without a load applies nothing again (the game kept them). An instant effect (runes, damage, poison) and one whose time ran out before the load never come back. The memory effects keep their time left the same way: nothing is written while the player is not loaded, and the loading exit writes the state again. They are not held by the seed-pack gate, though: the writes need no regulation row, so the running ones are still written and refreshed under an outdated seed pack (a reroll during the run); only a new one waits there, like any effect, since it starts when applied. A wrong save gets the memory defaults once, on its first loaded frame, and no refresh after; the effects keep their time left (the play clock stops there) and the race save's next loading exit writes them back. A game restart (a crash, an alt-F4) forgets the timed effects, the memory effects and the ink, pending, held or running, with their reminder rows: the mod's state dies with the game.

**Timed application.** A timed application, fresh or applied again, carries its time left, which the mod writes into the game's entry of the SpEffect. It first reads the row's timing from SpEffectParam (`race_items::speffect_timing`): its own `effectEndurance`, its `motionInterval` (the cycle) and, when its `cycleOccurrenceSpEffectId` names a row, that row's endurance, the tail (the cycled row, applied again every cycle while the row runs, outlasts it by its own endurance). Then it plans the row and the timer (`core::timed_effects::timed_apply`): a time left within the tail's endurance plus one cycle applies the tail row alone, with that time capped at the tail's endurance (a row left with less than a cycle could run out before its first cycled application, losing the whole tail); more applies the row with the time left minus the tail's, capped at the row's own endurance; a tail without endurance counts as none. The Mushroom's row 1450826 (15 s, a 0.5 s cycle) cycles vanilla 102360 (15 s after its last application): 30 s left applies 1450826 as it is, 25 s gives it 10 s, 15.3 s applies 102360 alone for its 15 s, 10 s applies 102360 alone for 10 s. The timer is the entry's removal timer, found by walking the player's active SpEffect list (`sp_effect_apply::set_speffect_timer`: PlayerIns + 0x178 is the `SpecialEffect`, its head entry at + 0x8; an entry holds its param id at + 0x8, the next entry at + 0x30 and the seconds left, an f32, at + 0x40; from fromsoftware-rs `cs/sp_effect.rs` and the TGA v1.17.0 table's "Active Effects", unverified by this mod in game until the lot's checks; walked on 1.17.x only, see Memory writes for what another version does), at most 256 entries, every read after PlayerIns and the write fault-safe (PlayerIns itself is read raw, as the apply reads it). An entry already there (a hit while the same effect runs) only gets its timer; otherwise the row is applied, then its entry's timer written, unless the row's own endurance already is the time left (within 0.25 s: a fresh hit). Timing unreadable (params not indexed yet, row missing): the row is applied as it is, with no timer write (not even into an entry already there), and runs its own duration. The Hammer gets its rate written first, then is applied the same way; an instant effect is applied as its row says. The mod log names the path that ran, each line of a timed one with `speffect_id` (the item's row), `row` (the one applied), `left_ms`, `timer_s` (the timer planned) and the `timing` read (a timing unreadable names `speffect_id` and `left_ms` alone); an instant one's lines name `speffect_id` alone:

- `[ITEMS] SpEffect timer set`: the entry was there, only its timer written.
- `[ITEMS] SpEffect applied`: applied, with nothing to write (a fresh hit).
- `[ITEMS] SpEffect applied, timer set`: applied, then its timer written (an effect applied again after a load).
- warning `[ITEMS] SpEffect applied, timer not found (runs its full row duration)`: applied, but no entry of that row was found to write the timer into.
- warning `[ITEMS] SpEffect applied, timing unreadable (runs its full row duration)`: the row's timing could not be read; applied as it is, no timer written.
- warning `[ITEMS] SpEffect list longer than the walk bound, timer not set`: the player's active list goes on past its 256th entry; the walk stops there, so an entry further on gets no timer (the apply, or `timer not found`, follows).
- warning `[ITEMS] SpEffect not applied`: the apply function or the player was not found.

**Memory writes.** The shell (`eldenring/memory_effects.rs`, called from `RaceTracker::apply_memory_state`) writes a field while it holds an effect (on each state the machine sends, against the game resetting it) and once when it falls back to its default after an effect was written there; a default is never written over and over. A failed write keeps the last value written, so the next state the machine sends tries again. Once none is live the machine sends the defaults once and nothing more, so the shell compares, each frame, the state the machine last sent (`RaceMachine::memory_state_sent`) with what it wrote: while they differ (a write that failed, or a batch lost to a frame that panicked, which `render` skips whole) it writes that state again, on a loaded frame, once a second has passed since the last write of any state (so never beside the machine's own 250 ms refresh of a live effect) (`RaceTracker::retry_memory_state`, `[ITEMS] Memory state written again`). When tracking stops for good (300 panicking frames in a row), the defaults are written one last time (`restore_memory_defaults`): the camera rows live until the game exits. The render bit, the scale and the chr set walk are written on 1.17.x only (`race_items::layout_known`, the versions their offsets were checked on; one warning per process elsewhere, `[ITEMS] Game version outside 1.17.x`), as are a timed SpEffect's timer and the junk weapon: on another version those effects do nothing in the game, their banner and reminder still showing. Two more follow from it there: a first-person Camera is left out whole (its rows are not written: the camera would sit inside a body the render bit no longer hides), and a timed SpEffect is not applied again after a load when it has less time left than its row's own duration (without its timer it would run that whole duration at every load; warning `[ITEMS] SpEffect not applied again`), so it ends at that load, its reminder row still counting down. The foes are walked only when `walk_foes`. Every read is fault-safe (`ReadProcessMemory`), except PlayerIns itself (read as the SpEffect apply reads it) and the LockCamParam rows, read and written in process (volatile) through libeldenring's params. Each write logs at info only when the value in the game changed (the first write, the restore, or a refresh after the game reset it), so the same line again in the middle of an effect means the game reset that field. Sources and confidence:

- **Player hidden**: bit 3 (`enable_render`) of PlayerIns + 0x1C5, alone (fromsoftware-rs `ChrInsFlags1c5`, Hexinton "ToggleDraw", Crowd Control's invisibility; high confidence on the bit, unverified whether the game sets it back on its own). The game writes bit 1 of that byte every frame from its own thread, so the bit is changed with an atomic or / and on the byte, once a fault-safe read proved it readable. Log `[ITEMS] Player render bit written` (`shown`, `byte_before`).
- **Scale**: ChrCtrl (PlayerIns + 0x58) + 0x2D4, three f32 (x, y, z), 1 by default and restored to 1 (fromsoftware-rs `ChrCtrl.scale_size_*`, Hexinton, Crowd Control; high on the offsets, unverified that it is visual only). Log `[ITEMS] Player scale written` (`before`, `scale`).
- **Animation speed**: libeldenring's `animation_speed` (PlayerIns + 0x190, + 0x28, + 0x17C8, f32, 1 by default: the character's whole animation rate; high on the chain, medium on what it slows) and its `torrent_animation_speed` (the same field on Torrent, the first entry of the summon buddy set embedded at WCM + 0x10F90, where the game puts its horses, Torrent first in a solo world (read in the 1.17.0 executable, checked by H.4 below); 1.17.x only, another version falls back to libeldenring's `torrent_animation_speed`, whose group lookup and static slot, the third chr set holder, can land on another character; a speed outside 0.01 to 100 is left alone), so a slowed runner does not escape on horseback; the restore writes 1 on both. Torrent not loaded is skipped, at debug level. Logs `[ITEMS] Player animation speed written` and `[ITEMS] Torrent animation speed written` (`before`, `speed`).
- **Camera**: `camDistTarget`, `chrOrgOffset_Y` and `camFovY` of every LockCamParam row (166 rows on the vanilla 1.17.0 regulation: row 0 reads as the normal on-foot camera; lock-on targets, scripted arenas and maybe Torrent use others), through libeldenring's in-memory params (Crowd Control writes row 0 the same way, the TGA table's custom FOV every row; high on the fields, medium-high that a row write applies live). A row's own values are saved the first time it is written (the params live until the game exits, so a race restart keeps what was written), every write derives from them, so a variant after another never stacks, and the default writes them back and forgets them. First person is `chrOrgOffset_Y` 1.5 and `camDistTarget` 0.1 with the player hidden; upside down is `camFovY` 300 (past 180 deg the image should flip: inferred, not observed); wide is `camFovY` 120 (Crowd Control's values). Logs `[ITEMS] Camera rows saved` (`saved`), `[ITEMS] Camera rows written` (`variant`, `rows`, `changed`: rows whose values differed), `[ITEMS] Camera rows restored` (`restored`); warning `[ITEMS] Camera rows not written` while the params are not indexed yet (`prewarm_params`).
- **Foes**: one bounded walk over WorldChrMan's chr sets, at most once a second (`core/foes.rs` decides, the shell reads and writes): the set count at WCM + 0x1CC58 (at most 196 read) and as many chr set holders from WCM + 0x1CC60 (one read for both, 0x18 bytes each, the ChrSet pointer first: the game keeps its holders packed in set index order, while its `chr_sets` array at WCM + 0x1DED8 is indexed by the set's index, with holes, so its first `count` slots are not the sets), each set's capacity at + 0x10 (at most 4096 entries read) and its entries at + 0x18 (one read per set, 0x10 bytes each, the ChrIns pointer first). Every non-null entry counts, whatever its load status: the game rebuilds its own list of the characters it updates each frame from the same holders and entries (WCM + 0x1F1B0 on 1.17.0 and 1.17.1, reading no load status), while the byte fromsoftware-rs names the load status (entry + 0x08) is not 2, `Active`, on most characters in the world (a walk that required it met one character among some 1600 slots, enemies around). That list is not read itself: refilled every frame, a read from the render thread could catch it half built. The player, ghost, summon buddy and debug chr sets (WCM + 0x10EE0, + 0x10F38, + 0x10F90, + 0x10FE8: the players, the phantoms, the player's horses and spirit ashes (Torrent its first entry) and debug spawns, no foes there) are left out, and the player and Torrent by address on any other set. The writes of a set are planned from one read of each character's byte, then the set's entries are read once more right before them: a character no longer on the set (freed since, its address maybe another object's) is left alone and counted `gone`. Hiding clears bit 3 on the characters that have it set and records each once its write worked (`FoesWalk::written`); showing sets it back only on recorded characters met again with it cleared and bit 7 (`death_flag`, the game killing or removing it) clear, then empties the record, so a character the game hides on purpose, or is removing (a dissolving enemy, a boss changing phase, an NPC leaving), stays hidden. The record follows the characters through loads: each hide walk that read every set rebuilds it from what it met, the characters it hid and the recorded ones it met still hidden, so an enemy hidden before a death or a warp that keeps its map block stays recorded and is drawn at the end, and a recorded character it did not meet is dropped as gone (its address may come back as another character). A walk that missed a set (its head or entries unreadable, past the capacity bound, or the set count changed during the read) keeps the recorded characters it did not meet. Every offset sits below PlayerIns (WCM + 0x1E508), the same on 1.17.0 and 1.17.2 (fromsoftware-rs `cs/world_chr_man.rs`, checked in the 1.17.0 executable; the fields after PlayerIns moved between the two and none is used); the walk runs on 1.17.x only (warning `[ITEMS] Foes walk skipped` otherwise). Logs `[ITEMS] Foes walked` at each walk (`hide`, `set_count` read at WCM + 0x1CC58, `sets` read, `skipped` sets (head or entries unreadable), entry `slots` read, `visited` characters, `unreadable`, `gone`, `changed`, `recorded`, `torrent_found`); warning `[ITEMS] Chr sets past the capacity bound, read up to it`. Tracy span `race_items.walk_foes`.
- warnings `[ITEMS] Player render bit not written`, `[ITEMS] Player scale not written`, `[ITEMS] Player animation speed not written`: the player was unreadable; the next state tries again, and a failed restore every second until it holds (none of the first two on a game version outside 1.17.x, said once at the first write).

**Equipment.** The steal, the gift and the junk weapon touch the player's equipment once, on the frame that applies the effect (a loaded frame of the race save, as every effect), never per frame: the Tracy spans `steal_talisman`, `race_items.spawn_talisman` and `equip_change.equip_junk_weapon` (with `equip_change.change_equipment` inside). Sources and confidence:

- **Talismans worn**: ChrAsm slots 17 to 20 of `equipment_param_ids` (`PlayerGameData + 0x398 + 4 * slot`, one read of four i32 at PlayerGameData + 0x3DC), raw EquipParamAccessory ids, -1 for an empty slot (fromsoftware-rs `ChrAsmSlot`; the ids the equip load reads; high confidence, unverified by this mod in game until the checks). The removal is the seed's event for that talisman, raised by its flag (`EventFlagReader::set_flag`): it empties the slot and drops the effect, takes a talisman held but not worn alike, and survives a reload (validated in game); the mod writes no equipment. Logs `[ITEMS] Talisman removal flag raised` (`equipped`: the four slots read, `roll`, `talisman_id`, `flag`) or `[ITEMS] No worn talisman in the table, nothing taken` (`equipped`), then the machine's `[ITEMS] Talisman stolen` or `[ITEMS] Nothing to steal`; warnings `[ITEMS] Talisman slots unreadable, nothing taken` and `[ITEMS] Talisman removal flag not written, nothing taken` (both reported not landed).
- **Talisman given**: the goods' give (`item_spawner::inject_one`, `func_item_inject`) with the talisman category nibble, `0x20000000 | talisman_id` (high confidence: the call the goods use, another category). Logs `func_item_inject called` (`encoded`), then `[ITEMS] Talisman given`; warning `[ITEMS] Talisman not given (the give function or MapItemMan is unavailable)`.
- **Junk weapon**: the game's own `EquipGameData::ChangeEquipment`, the function the equipment menu equips through (`eldenring/equip_change.rs`; RVA 0x249160 in the 1.16.2, 1.17.0, 1.17.1 and 1.17.2 executables, the same body in all four; the TGA v1.17.0 table's `equipGear`). The mod finds it by a 16-byte pattern with no wildcard, unique in those four, which ends on `add rcx, 0x6C` (the ChrAsm offset, so a layout move fails the scan instead of running with stale offsets); the entry is 0x17 bytes before the match and is kept only when its prologue is the expected one. It is resolved on a background thread with the SpEffect apply function (`PrewarmRaceItems`). It takes seven arguments, the last three bools on the stack, all declared in its type (an undeclared stack argument was the phantom skin regression), and the mod passes what the menu passes: `(PlayerGameData + 0x2B0, slot, &handle, index, true, true, false)`. It writes the slot's gaitem handle through the game's reference counting, its param id, the arm style (one-handed when the slot is in hand: two-handing ends, the left hand's too), the bolt state, the inventory index and the equipment entry. A raw write cannot keep the handle's reference count, so there is none: a game version where the function is not found gives nothing (a junk weapon given but never equipped would be a free item). Steps: (1) the function resolved and the player loaded; (2) `right_weapon_slot` (PlayerGameData + 0x32C) gives the slot, `1 + 2 * right_weapon_slot`, whose state is logged: inventory index (`+ 0x2B8 + 4 * slot`), gaitem handle (`+ 0x340 + 4 * slot`), param id (`+ 0x398 + 4 * slot`), equipment entry (`+ 0x5F8 + 4 * slot`) and arm style (+ 0x324, 0 empty, 1 one-handed, 2 left two-handed, 3 right two-handed); a hand already holding that weapon stops there, with nothing given; (3) the handles of that weapon in the held normal list; (4) the give; (5) the new copy (`new_copy`, at whatever upgrade level it landed), whose inventory index is the key list's capacity (InventoryItemsData + 0x14, 384 in practice) plus its position (`core::item_inventory::normal_index`); none found (a full inventory: the game may put the weapon in storage, which cannot be equipped from) stops there, the weapon given; (6) the call; (7) the slot read back. Confidence: the function, its arguments and the offsets verified in code (the 1.17.0 executable, cross-checked on the other three); the synchronous give and the handle diff derived; the in-game result unverified until the checks (I below). Left out: what the menu does after the call (a quick slot fix-up, a session notification, a dirty byte), and an open equipment menu, which the mod cannot tell, does not defer the equip.

- `[ITEMS] Resolved ChangeEquipment` (`addr`, `rva`): once per process, at the first race with items; `rva` is `0x249160` on 1.17.x. Warnings `[ITEMS] ChangeEquipment pattern absent or ambiguous in eldenring.exe, junk weapons disabled` and `[ITEMS] ChangeEquipment prologue not the expected one, junk weapons disabled`: this game version gets no junk weapon.
- `[ITEMS] Junk weapon slot before` (`right_weapon_slot`, `slot`, `state`: `index`, `handle`, `param_id`, `entry_id`, `arm_style`).
- `[ITEMS] Junk weapon already in hand, nothing given`: the slot already holds that weapon, at any upgrade level.
- `[ITEMS] Inventory list headers` before and after the give, `func_item_inject called` between them.
- `[ITEMS] Junk weapon copy found` (`handle`, `copy_id`: the copy's item id, the weapon id plus the upgrade level it landed at, `had`: the handles of that weapon before the give, at any level, `position`, `key_capacity`, `index`).
- `[ITEMS] Junk weapon ChangeEquipment called` (`slot`, `handle`, `index`).
- `[ITEMS] Junk weapon read back, as expected` (`state`: the copy's `index` and `handle`, its `copy_id` as `param_id` and `entry_id`, `arm_style=1`); warning `[ITEMS] Junk weapon read back differs from the equip` (`expected_index`, `expected_handle`, `expected_arm_style`, `state`), followed by `[ITEMS] Junk weapon not in hand` unless only the arm style differs.
- warnings `[ITEMS] Junk weapon skipped, nothing given: ...` (ChangeEquipment unresolved, the player not loaded, PlayerGameData, the right weapon slot, the slot or the inventory unreadable), `[ITEMS] Junk weapon not given: ...` (the give function or MapItemMan unavailable), `[ITEMS] Junk weapon given, not equipped: ...` (the inventory unreadable after the give; no new copy, with `last_add_item_result`, 4 for a full inventory; an inventory index out of range) and `[ITEMS] Junk weapon read-back failed: slot unreadable`, each followed by `[ITEMS] Junk weapon not in hand`.

**Hunter.** The spawn, the looks and the end (`eldenring/hunter.rs`, its pure parts in `core/hunter_spawn.rs`) run only when the machine asks: a request at a time, looks every 250 ms for the 3 s after it and once a second while he hunts and for 10 s after the last miss, an end; never per frame (Tracy spans `race_items.request_hunter`, `race_items.find_hunter` and `race_items.end_hunter`). No pointer to him is kept between calls: each look and each end walk the debug chr set again. Every read after WorldChrMan is fault-safe and every write goes through `WriteProcessMemory` to an address checked just before, so an end on a frame that is not loaded (the run ends during a loading screen) logs what it could not read and writes nothing there. The character debug flags are never used. Sources and confidence:

- **Creator**: `CS::CSDebugChrCreator` at `[WorldChrMan + 0x1E648]` on 1.17.0 and 1.17.1, `[WorldChrMan + 0x1E658]` on 1.17.2 (`core::hunter_spawn::creator_offset`; a static scan of the three executables, where the WorldChrMan constructor stores it and its update calls it, and the fromsoftware-rs bindings of 1.17.1 and 1.17.2 agree; high confidence). The field moves between builds, so any other build gets no Hunter: each request there is a miss. The creator is trusted only once its RTTI name reads `.?AVCSDebugChrCreator@CS@@` (its vftable, the complete object locator right before it, the type descriptor's RVA at locator + 0xC, the name at descriptor + 0x10; Crowd Control checks it the same way).
- **Request**: written at these offsets from the creator, in this order (fromsoftware-rs, Crowd Control's writer and the Hexinton table agree; high): the model name `c<chr_id>` in UTF-16 at 0x100, the NpcParam row at 0xF0, the NpcThinkParam row at 0xF4, the event entity id 0 at 0xF8, the talk id 0 at 0xFC, the enemy type 0 (u8) at 0x178, the CharaInitParam row -1 at 0x17C, the spawn point at 0xB0, then the spawn byte at 0x44 set to 1, last: the game reads the fields once it sees it, on its own WorldChrMan update, a frame or more later. Refused while the byte is not 0 (another spawn pending). A live character of his row already on the debug chr set (a late spawn of an earlier request) counts as the spawn, with nothing written: never a second Margit. The team type is never forced (Margit's row says 7, Boss).
- **Placement**: 3 m behind the player along their facing, 0.5 m up (`core::hunter_spawn::placement`), from the player's physics module (PlayerIns + 0x190, + 0x68): its position at + 0x70 (the Havok frame the creator takes, libeldenring's `chunk_position`; high) and its orientation quaternion at + 0x50, the facing `(sin yaw, 0, cos yaw)` with `yaw = 2 atan2(q.y, q.w)` (derived from the practice tool's enemy angle; the sign is checked by J.5).
- **Finding him**: the debug chr set embedded at WorldChrMan + 0x10FE8, the same on every 1.17.x (capacity at + 0x10, entries at + 0x18, read as the foes walk reads a set; TheHunters finds its spawns there; high), a character whose NpcParam row (ChrIns + 0x60, his clone row is his alone) is his, with HP above 0: the data module (ChrIns + 0x190, + 0), HP at + 0x138 and max HP at + 0x13C, the chain libeldenring reads the player's HP through (high). An HP that cannot be read is no sign he is gone: a look keeps a Hunter already seen hunting (one never seen is not seen yet), so a misread never ends a hunt without his end; the end writes 0 HP only over an HP read above 0 (`core::hunter_spawn::found_by_a_look` and `alive`).
- **Rows**: before each request, his NpcParam row (`npc_param_id`) and NpcThinkParam row (`think_id`) are looked up by id in the loaded regulation through libeldenring's in-memory params (`race_items::param_has_row`, the ids alone): a seed built by a FogModWrapper older than his rows carries their ids in graph.json but not the rows, and the game is never asked for a row it lacks; that request is a miss (high).
- **End**: a spawn byte still at 1 goes back to 0 (the creator verified first), then every live character of his row gets 0 HP at `[[ChrIns + 0x190] + 0] + 0x138` (Crowd Control's kill; that it plays his death and hands nothing is derived, J.8), each one's entry read again right before its write (the same pointer in the same entry, with his row) or skipped.

- `[ITEMS] Hunter creator offset` (`exe_version`, `offset`): once per process, at the first request. Warning `[ITEMS] Hunter disabled: no debug character creator offset for this game version` on any other build.
- `[ITEMS] Hunter creator` (`offset`, `rtti`, `spawn_flag`, `manipulator`, `spawn_count`, `enemy_type`, `last_created`): each request, once the RTTI name matched; warning `[ITEMS] Hunter creator RTTI name differs` (`offset`, `name`) otherwise.
- `[ITEMS] Hunter placement` (`player`, `yaw_deg`, `spawn`).
- `[ITEMS] Hunter request written` (`chr_id`, `npc_param_id`, `think_id`, `stale`: the dead characters of his row still on the debug chr set).
- `[ITEMS] Hunter already on the debug chr set, no new request` (`alive`).
- warnings `[ITEMS] Hunter not requested: ...` (a row missing from the regulation, with `param` and `row`: NpcParam `npc_param_id` or NpcThinkParam `think_id`, a seed built by a stale FogModWrapper, see Deploy; the params not indexed yet; the player not loaded; WorldChrMan, the spawn byte, the debug chr set or the player's position unreadable; the creator not verified, with `reason`; another spawn pending; the position not plausible; no model name; a creator write failed), each a miss (`[ITEMS] Hunter spawn refused, counted as a miss`).

- `[ITEMS] Hunter spawned` (`chr`, `after_ms`, `npc_param_id`, `event_id`, `team`, `hp`: HP and max HP, `last_created_match`, `pos`, `dist` to the player, `alive`): the first look that sees him; debug `[ITEMS] Hunter not seen yet` before it.

- `[ITEMS] Hunter lost` (`reason`: `dead` when his row's characters on the set are all dead, `absent` when none is there; `since_seen_ms`): the first look that no longer sees him, then the machine's `[ITEMS] Hunter gone (a load or a kill removed him), the hunt is over`.
- `[ITEMS] Quit-out during a hunt: the Hunter comes back once the player settles`: at the loading exit of a quit-out while he hunts or is being brought back; his return then logs as any request (`Hunter spawn requested`, `Hunter request written`, `Hunter spawned`), with no `Hunter found, he hunts`. `[ITEMS] A load on the Hunter's way back ends the hunt`: any other loading exit during that return, then `Hunter ended`.

- `[ITEMS] Hunter killed` (`chr`, `hp_before`, `runes`): the end of a Hunter seen. `[ITEMS] Hunter late arrival killed` (`chr`, `hp_before`, `after_ms`): one of a request never seen, arrived after the last look or during the 10 s watch after the last miss.
- `[ITEMS] Hunter kill skipped` (`chr`, `reason=entry_changed`); warning `[ITEMS] Hunter kill failed: HP unwritable`.
- `[ITEMS] Hunter end done` (`killed`, `skipped`, `dead`): each end that read the set.
- warnings `[ITEMS] Hunter look failed: ...` (WorldChrMan or the debug chr set unreadable; `kept=true`: a Hunter already seen keeps hunting, so his end still comes and kills him; otherwise not seen), `[ITEMS] Hunter end skipped: WorldChrMan unreadable`, `[ITEMS] Hunter end: creator not verified, a pending spawn not cleared` and `[ITEMS] Hunter end: debug chr set unreadable, nothing killed`. Warning `[ITEMS] Hunter end incomplete, he may remain` follows any end that could not read a step, skipped a kill or failed a write.

### Overlay

All sizes derive from the display size each frame (`core/item_banner.rs`); text is built on receipt, textures are loaded once at init, and the seconds left go through a reused buffer.

- **Banner**, 4 s of wall time, queued, fading in over 0.25 s and out over the last 0.5 s (`ItemFeed::banner_alpha`, applied to every colour it draws): top centre, block top at 13% of the screen height, over a gradient from the top edge (alpha 0.78) fading out at 48% of the height. Icon 7.5% of the width, title in uppercase Barlow Condensed 600 at 3.4% (letter spacing 0.08 em; danger colour for an effect received, gold otherwise), line in Public Sans at 1.6%, detail at 1.2% in the disabled text colour, each with a dark shadow.
- **Held row**: while the player holds an item, any item, a first row in the race panel, above the reminder rows, shows its icon and gold name, with no countdown. The held item is the one `auth_ok.held_item` names, then the last grant's; an `item_result`, which the server sends once it spent the item, forgets it. The row appears when the grant's goods is in the inventory (spawned, or already there; a new grant replaces it), follows every inventory read (shown while the held item's goods is in the inventory, so it comes back after a game restart), leaves on its goods' `item_remove`, a use of its goods or a new start, and stops showing once the run is over (finished or out). The name and icon come from the catalog (`race_item_catalog.items`).
- **Reminder rows**, in the race panel after the leaderboard and before the status message: icon, text (danger for an effect received, gold for the sender's own) and the seconds left, right aligned. They count play time: a load freezes them.
- No overlay tag marks a race with items: the race panel has no room to spare.
- **Ink**: while active (its `duration_ms` of play time, 30 s on a graph.json 4.13 seed), every exit row reads `-> ???` with no location line. The exits cache is rebuilt when the zone, the width or the ink state changes, not every frame. At the hit, the zone that decides (the one a pending reveal brings, else the current zone) makes one of three things of it (`RaceMachine::ink_start`). A zone with a `zone_update.unused_exits` of 0 (every fog there leads on, so hiding them costs the runner nothing) makes it wait, as a pending ink (`[ITEMS] Ink waits for a zone with unused exits`): its reminder row (`Exits hidden`) shows `pending` where the seconds go, so it does not read as the Ink the runner holds, and the exits stay readable. A boss arena whose count is not 0 (unused exits, or a count unknown) and whose boss lives (its `zone_update.defeat_flag`, one of the seed's `boss_flags`, not killed yet; the final boss's arena carries none) holds it (`[ITEMS] Ink held, exits hidden until the boss dies`): the exits are hidden at once, through the fight, and its row shows its whole time without counting down; the kill runs it from there (`[ITEMS] Ink started` with `by="boss killed"`, as soon as a flag scan reads the defeat flag set: the boss flags rotate, 8 per 100 ms scan). Any other zone runs it at once (`[ITEMS] Ink started`): one with unused exits or a count the mod does not know (`null`, a seed that predates 4.13), an arena whose boss is already dead included, and so does no zone revealed yet. The hit logs what it made of the ink (`by="hit"`, or `by="loading exit"`); a second hit on an ink not run yet logs nothing. Each zone revealed decides again for an ink not run yet, pending or held (`by="zone revealed"`), the same zone revealed again after a death or quit-out reload included: a held ink whose runner leaves the arena with its boss alive (a death, a respawn at a grace elsewhere) runs in a zone with unused exits and waits, readable again with its time whole, in one without. An ink applied on a loading exit frame always waits, whatever the zone, and the next reveal decides: that frame still shows the zone left behind. A zone query the server cannot resolve (its `zone_query_ack`: no `zone_update` comes, as after some death reloads) lets the current zone decide instead, unless a reveal is pending (`by="zone query unresolved"`). The `[RACE] Zone revealed` log line names the zone's `unused_exits` and `defeat_flag`. A kill read once the next zone's `zone_update` is in (a boss that warps the runner out on its death, read late by the flag rotation) leaves that zone to decide at its reveal. A second hit while pending or held changes nothing; while running, the later end wins. An ink not run yet, pending or held, goes with the run: finished or out, or a new start.

### Icons

`mod/assets/race_items/<icon>.png` (160x160 PNG) are embedded in the DLL and uploaded once at init; an icon name the mod does not know draws `unknown.png`. The art comes from the generator repo: drop the final `race_items_icon_<icon>.png` files (square, 768x768 or more: the generator derives the in-game textures from them and its `tools/import_race_item_icons.py` reframes delivered art) in `speedfog/data/plugins/`, run `uv run tools/sync_race_item_icons.py` (it reads the names from the catalog's `[icons]`, checks every file is a square PNG of at least 160x160, copies nothing when any is bad, and writes each icon downscaled to 160x160: the banner draws it at 7.5% of the screen width, 144 px at 1080p, and the full-size art would weigh megabytes in the DLL), then release the mod. The same run writes every icon at 64x64 into `web/static/race_items/` for the site's item list (drawn at 32 px). `unknown.png` is outside the catalog and edited by hand. `item_box.png` is synced with the others but not embedded: the item box is only used in game (the Race Item's inventory icon, see below), the overlay never draws it.

In game, the Race Item shows the held item's icon: the mod writes the item's `icon_id` into its goods' `EquipParamGoods` row in memory (`race_items::set_goods_icon`) before each grant's spawn, and for the held item again before every inventory report, at each loading exit and reconnection while connected (a game restart reloads the params; `auth_ok.held_item` names the item to the restarted mod). A write made before the params are indexed (`prewarm_params`) is skipped with a warning (`[ITEMS] Goods icon not set`), and the next one makes it. Before a first write it shows its own `item_box` icon (60396). The in-game textures come with the seed (icon ids 60385 to 60406: the seven lot 3 items take 60397 to 60403, the three lot 4 items 60404 to 60406).

## In-game test protocol

With the compiled mod and one game, on the local server. `tools/race_items_bot.py` plays the other runners: each bot is a local user whose mod is the script, so every item reaches you through the server's real rules. Where to look: the overlay, the mod log (`speedfog_racing.log` next to the DLL, `[ITEMS]` lines), the bot console, and `status` in that console (rank, tier, layer, held item of every runner).

### Setup

1. **Mod.** On Windows, `cd mod && cargo build --lib --release`, then copy `target/release/speedfog_racing.dll` over `tools/assets/speedfog_racing.dll` on the server machine: every seed zip the local server hands out carries that DLL (`MOD_DLL_PATH`).
2. **Server and web.** The dev server (`:8000`) and web (`:5173`) run; `WEBSOCKET_URL` in `server/.env` is the address the game PC reaches (the LAN address, not localhost).
3. **Seeds.** A pool folder `$SEEDS_POOL_DIR/race_items_test/` with a pool `config.toml` and race items seeds named `seed_<anything>.zip` (generated with `[plugin.race_items] enabled = true`, graph.json 4.12 or later), scanned with `cd server && uv run speedfog-scan-seeds --pool race_items_test`. Every race consumes a seed: add one copy per race under a new name and scan again.
4. **Race.** On the web, create a community race in that pool with Race items on, and join it.
5. **Bots.** `cd server && uv run python ../tools/race_items_bot.py <race_id> --bots <user1>,<user2>`, two local usernames; they join while the race is in setup and report ready. Keep the console open (`help` lists the commands). Two commands set how a bot answers the lot 4 effects, which wait for the target's `item_report`: `steal <bot> lands|nothing` (`lands`, the default, answers landed with the first id of the effect's `talismans` table; `nothing` answers not landed with no talisman, so the thief gets `Nothing to steal`) and `hunter <bot> lands|miss` (`lands`, the default, answers landed; `miss` answers not landed, so the sender gets `The Hunter lost the trail`). A bot that receives the `talisman` gift prints its id.
6. **Start.** Download your seed zip from the race page, launch it, wait until your mod is ready, then start the race from the web.

Positions decide the targets, so the checks run in this order. The bots report your IGT, so the item order (above) follows the layers here: a bot is never ahead of you in IGT, nor you of a bot. Wait a few seconds after the start before driving the bots: until its first report of the running race, a bot is not playing and the server drops its flags (the console says so). Then go through the first fog gate: you lead (checks B). Afterwards, `ahead <user1>` walks that bot forward to the deepest layer while `<user2>` stays at the start: the field reads user1, you, user2 for checks C to F, so you draw middle items, you are the runner just behind user1 and just ahead of user2, and group items from either bot hit you. `give WHO ITEM` hands any item to a bot or to you (yours spawns at your next load: a fog gate or a quit-out).

### Checks

#### A. Grants and inventory (any position)

1. Kill a boss in game: the item lands as the Race Item, in the same quick slot every time, with the item's icon, the gold banner and the held row (`[ITEMS] Goods spawned`, then `item_received`); `status` shows it confirmed.
2. Quit out and reload, then restart the game: still one copy, with the item's icon and the held row back; `[ITEMS] Inventory read` lists the Race Item and no new spawn is requested (the grant was confirmed). `Goods already held, spawn skipped` only shows when the server sends a grant again, after a crash between the spawn and its `item_received`.
3. Use it: the slot greys out at 0 and the next grant refills it; the next `[ITEMS] Inventory read` no longer lists it, the held row goes, `status` shows nothing held. A targeted item shows on the console as `hit by ...` on a bot.
4. `give <you> mushroom`, load, use it: rolls and sprints cost no stamina for about 30 s, with its buff icon and the "Infinite stamina" reminder counting down.
5. `give <you> rune_pouch_2`, load, use it: 20000 runes and the "Runes added" banner.
6. `give <you> shield` while you hold a Race Item, then load: the Race Item leaves the inventory (take back), the Shield arrives.
7. `give <you> banana` while you hold a Race Item, then load: still one copy, now with the Banana's icon and row (`Goods already held, spawn skipped`, then `item_received`); `status` shows it confirmed.

#### B. While you lead (before `ahead`)

1. Blue Shell from user2 (`give <user2> blue_shell`, `use <user2>`): max HP halved for 15 s, current HP capped, never killed.

#### C. Items on you (after `ahead <user1>`; `give <bot> ITEM`, then `use <bot>`)

1. Red Shell from user2: HP drops by 40% of max and stays down. Repeat at low HP: the hit lands at 20%, then deals 10%, and never kills (`[ITEMS] Damage applied`).
2. Banana from user1: 15% of max HP.
3. Hammer from either bot: fat roll for 15 s, "Fat-roll" reminder. The log line `[ITEMS] Equip load rate set` gives `current` and `max`: `current` must match the equipment screen's load, once with a bow and arrows equipped and once without (arrows and bolts are left out of the sum), and the screen's max becomes `current / 0.97`. A second Hammer within the 15 s keeps you heavy instead of cancelling the first.
4. Lightning: forced slow walk and half size for 15 s, then normal speed and size, with one `Slowed and shrunk` row.
5. Flask Stopper: no flask for 15 s.
6. Toxic Blossom: poisoned; Neutralizing Boluses cure it.
7. Ink, in a zone with unused exits: every exit reads `-> ???` without location for 30 s (15 s on a 4.12 seed, whose zones carry no count and start it at once, a live boss arena holding it until the kill), then comes back, its row counting down.
   Ink in a zone with no unused exit (a two-gate boss arena): the exits stay readable and an "Exits hidden" row shows `pending` instead of seconds (`[ITEMS] Ink waits for a zone with unused exits`), the boss kill included; a second Ink changes nothing; go through a fog gate into a zone with an unused exit: `[ITEMS] Ink started`, the exits read `???` and the row counts down from 30 s, also across a load (it stops while loading). Ink in a boss arena with an unused exit (Godskin Duo, Godfrey, Gideon, when the seed leaves one of their gates unused; `[RACE] Zone revealed` shows `unused_exits` above 0 and a `defeat_flag`), its boss alive: the exits read `???` at once and the row shows 30 without counting down through the fight (`[ITEMS] Ink held, exits hidden until the boss dies`); kill the boss: `[ITEMS] Ink started` with `by="boss killed"` and the row counts down from 30 s from the kill. Die to the boss while held: the respawn's zone decides (the arena revealed again holds it, its row still at 30; a zone with unused exits runs it; one without lets it wait, the exits readable).
8. Two items in a row (one per bot): the banners queue, 4 s each, each fading in and out, and the reminder rows stack.

#### D. Shield

1. `give <you> shield`, load: the Shield lands as its own goods, not in a quick slot, and a gold Shield row shows in the race panel, also after a game restart. Then a Red Shell from user2: the row goes, your Shield leaves the inventory (removal event), your banner reads "Blocked Red Shell" from user2, and the bot's result reads "Blocked by <you>'s shield" with no damage detail.

#### E. Robustness

1. Network pull: cut the game PC's network and wait until the race page shows your mod disconnected (the server only notices a dead socket after a while), then a bot uses a Red Shell on you: the bot's result reads `Missed` and nothing replays once you are back. Use your own item while cut off: once back, its use resolves once (the use flag is replayed).
2. Alt-F4 right after using an item: after the restart, the use resolves once, neither lost nor doubled.
3. Reset: a bot uses an item on you during a loading screen, the organizer resets the race before it ends: once the race starts again, the old effect is not applied. The reset closes every mod socket: the bots connect again, report ready and walk from the start node once the race runs again.
